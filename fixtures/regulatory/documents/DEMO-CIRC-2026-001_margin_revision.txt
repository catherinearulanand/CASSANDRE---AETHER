DEMO CLEARING NOTICE DEMO-CIRC-2026-001
Issued by: Northwind Demo Clearing (fictional). SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT FOR AETHER TESTING - NOT LAW.

1. Purpose
1.1 This notice revises the synthetic margin policy for index futures cleared on DEMO_NSE.

2. Margin multiplier
2.1 With effect from 1 January 2027, the clearing member shall apply a margin multiplier of 1.25 times the base margin to index futures accounts on DEMO_NSE.
2.2 Until that date the margin multiplier of 1.00 times the base margin continues to apply.

3. Collateral coverage
3.1 The clearing member shall hold eligible collateral of at least 100 per cent of required margin at all times.
3.2 Eligible collateral is determined in accordance with Annexure A.

4. Position limits
4.1 A client shall not exceed 100 lots of net position in a single index futures contract.
4.2 For paragraph 4.1, positions are aggregated per client classification.

Annexure A
A.1 Eligible collateral comprises cash and the synthetic instruments listed in the clearing schedule.
